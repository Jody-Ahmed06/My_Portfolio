About me

A puzzle-solver at heart

Ever since I was a kid, I've loved puzzles — not just solving them, but finding the bigger picture hidden inside the tiny pieces. That curiosity grew into a passion for making sense of complex software architectures.

Later, I discovered how much I enjoy uncovering edge cases and turning what feels "unpredictable" into something secure, reliable, and logical. That's what led me to Software Quality Assurance.

For me, QA is more than just finding bugs — it's about storytelling with logic. Whether I'm analyzing an SRS document, testing deep API endpoints, or tracing embedded system flows, I bring both engineering logic and creative problem-solving into my work to ensure systems truly serve their users.

Alhamdulillah for every lesson learned on this journey.
